8. Explicitly silent video: no default narrator inserted.
9. Voice complaint after generation: inspect complete-profile equality and contradictory instructions first. If retry authorized, vary voice wording only; preserve original story, visuals, words, music and provider settings. Do not mark success without listening evidence.
